## 📂 Files in this folder

- **Bat.md** — The bat line: the roster's most defensible flavour claim and its least defensible category claim, since fruit bats chew the cherry and spit the bean rather than digesting it, leaving the product a fermentation story rather than a digestion one.
- **Capybara.md** — The capybara: the roster's best operational performer. Cooperative, unbothered, predictable output, and the only supplier whose retrieval procedure has never produced an incident report. The basis of the house blend.
- **Civet.md** — The Asian palm civet as the category incumbent behind Kopi Luwak: proven price point, proven customer, and the caged-production scandals that define the ethical floor every other animal in the roster is measured against.
- **Elephant.md** — The elephant line (the Black Ivory model): the best flavour profile in the roster, produced by fermentation in a herbivore gut, undermined by a yield ratio of roughly 33 to 1 and the retrieval problem that ratio creates.
- **Goat.md** — The goat line as the company's heritage product, tied to the Kaldi origin legend in which goats discovered coffee, and sold on that story despite an unremarkable cup and a goat that will eat the packaging as readily as the cherry.
- **Honey-Badger.md** — The honey badger line: highest measured caffeine in the roster and the only supplier that has been suspended on safety grounds, following three incidents in one quarter and a facilities bill for a reinforced enclosure.
- **Ostrich.md** — The ostrich line: a bird that swallows stones to grind its food and therefore returns coffee pre-ground, a genuine processing saving that is partly cancelled by inconsistent particle size and the need to screen grit out of every batch.
- **Penguin.md** — Full post-mortem of the abandoned penguin line: the cold-brew pitch, a feeding trial in which eleven of fourteen birds refused the cherry outright, the caffeine problem caused by the three that did not, the tasting panel that resigned rather than drink the result, and the control batch with no penguin in it that tasted the same.
- **Sloth.md** — The sloth line: a genuinely long fermentation caused by the slowest digestion of any mammal, which produces a distinctive deep flavour and a two-week supply lag that makes demand forecasting nearly impossible.
