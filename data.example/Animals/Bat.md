# Bat

Our best coffee, and a product we cannot quite describe honestly in our own category.

## What actually happens

Fruit bats go for the ripe cherry on the plant. They chew the pulp, take the sugar, and
drop or spit the bean, usually still on the farm. Saliva and the open-air ferment that
follows do the rest.

Nothing is digested. The bean does not pass through the bat.

## Why we sell it anyway

Because it is excellent. The bat selects for ripeness better than any human picker, works
at night for free, and the short enzymatic ferment gives a bright, floral, honeyed cup
that has beaten everything else in blind cupping twice running.

## The category problem

Our entire brand is "coffee that has been through an animal". This coffee has been *near*
an animal, with enthusiasm.

Marketing wanted "bat-digested". Legal said no, correctly, and the compromise wording
"bat-selected, wild-fermented" is in [[Brand/Tasting-Notes]]. The debate is logged in
[[Meetings/Decisions]], ADR-4.

The irony that our most honest product required the most careful labelling has been noted
by everyone, repeatedly, at length.
