# Capybara

Our best supplier, and the quiet reason the company still exists.

## Why the capybara wins

Nothing about the capybara is impressive on paper. The flavour is good, not remarkable:
mild, nutty, clean finish, slightly sweet. It will not win a cupping against
[[Animals/Elephant]].

What it wins on is every other column.

| Factor | Capybara | Roster average |
|---|---|---|
| Yield ratio | 4:1 | 12:1 |
| Retrieval hours per kg | 3 | 11 |
| Incidents to date | 0 | 2.4 |
| Willingness to be approached | total | varies |

The capybara is a large, calm, semi-aquatic rodent with no apparent objection to being
part of a supply chain. It eats the cherry. It sits in the water. It produces, on a
schedule, in a predictable location. Birds sit on it. It does not mind.

## Commercial position

The capybara is the base of our house blend and roughly 70% of revenue. Everything else
on the roster is a halo product that exists to make the capybara look reasonably priced.

See [[Market/Pricing]] for the blend margin and [[Brand/Naming]] for the naming fight we
had over this line.

## Risk

Single-supplier concentration. If the capybara arrangement fails we do not have a
business, we have a press clipping. [[Operations/Supply-Chain]] tracks this as the
company's only red risk.
