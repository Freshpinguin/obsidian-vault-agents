# Civet

The Asian palm civet. The animal that made this category exist, and the reason we have a
price anchor at all.

## Why it matters to us

The civet proved three things we now depend on:

1. A customer will pay 600 EUR per kilogram for coffee an animal has eaten.
2. They will do so repeatedly, and tell people.
3. The phrase "passed through the digestive tract" can appear on a label without
   collapsing the sale.

## Why we do not farm civets

The industry went bad. Demand outran the number of wild civets willing to cooperate, so
producers caged them and force-fed them cherry. The coffee got worse and the story got
much worse.

**Decision.** We do not keep civets. See [[Meetings/Decisions]], ADR-2. Our roster is
built on animals that can be fed on their own terms, which is also the only arrangement
[[Legal/Regulatory]] will sign off on.

## Tasting baseline

Earthy, low acidity, syrupy body. Honestly, somewhat flat. Several of our cuppings have
beaten it blind, which is the entire commercial opportunity and also the thing we are not
allowed to say in marketing. See [[Brand/Tasting-Notes]].
