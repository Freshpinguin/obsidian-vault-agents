# Elephant

Our premium line, and the clearest example of a product that works and an operation that
barely does.

## The flavour

Genuinely excellent. The elephant is a herbivore with a long, slow, plant-fermenting gut,
and the bean comes out with the bitterness stripped and something closer to chocolate and
tamarind left behind. It is the only animal in our roster that beats the
[[Animals/Civet]] baseline without an argument.

## The yield ratio

This is the problem.

| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| 33 kg of coffee cherry fed | ~1 kg of sellable bean |

The elephant chews. The elephant is also enormous, and much of what goes in is destroyed
on the way through rather than fermented. Everything else in the herd's diet continues as
normal, at volume, which makes the retrieval problem described in
[[Operations/Retrieval]] a genuinely physical one.

## Unit economics

At 33:1 the cherry cost alone puts us above 1,000 EUR per kilogram before a single hour
of labour. This line exists for the price ceiling and the press, not the margin. See
[[Market/Pricing]].

## Operational notes

- One handler is never enough. Two minimum, by policy, after the incident logged in
  [[Legal/Incidents]].
- Collection happens on the herd's schedule. There is no negotiating this.
