# Goat

Our heritage line. Sold almost entirely on a story that predates the entire category.

## The legend

The Ethiopian origin story: a goatherd named Kaldi noticed his goats dancing after eating
the red cherries of a certain shrub, tried them himself, and coffee followed. Whether any
of it happened is unknowable, but it means the goat has a claim no other animal on our
roster has — the goat was here first.

It is the only line where the story is older than the product, which makes it the easiest
thing we sell.

## The cup

Unremarkable. Grassy, thin, a little sour. Our cupping panel scores it last, consistently,
below even the [[Animals/Civet]] baseline.

We sell it anyway, in small heritage tins, at a price the cup does not justify. Nobody who
buys it is buying it to drink. See [[Market/Customer-Research]] for the segment this
serves, which is almost entirely gifts.

## Operational note

A goat will eat the cherry. A goat will also eat the feed sack, the labels, the clipboard
and the retrieval log. Nothing stays on the ground near a goat.

Our first batch was lost this way and is recorded in [[Legal/Incidents]].
