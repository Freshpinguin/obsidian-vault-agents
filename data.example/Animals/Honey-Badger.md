# Honey Badger

Status: **suspended**, pending a facilities review that keeps not happening.

## What was promising

The honey badger has an aggressive metabolism and an extremely robust gut, and the beans
came back with the highest measured caffeine content of anything on the roster, around
40% above our capybara baseline. The flavour was sharp, almost peppery. A genuine
espresso product.

Two customers independently described the cup as "confrontational". Both reordered.

## What went wrong

The honey badger is famous for not caring. This is charming in a documentary and
expensive in a supply chain.

- It does not accept a feeding schedule. It accepts whatever it can reach.
- It opened the feed store twice. The second time it was not even the feed store, it was
  the wall next to the feed store.
- Retrieval requires approaching the animal. It does not want to be approached and has
  made this point with total clarity.

All three incidents in Q3 involved this supplier. See [[Legal/Incidents]].

## Current position

Suspended by the safety review, not by the product team, who would like it noted that the
coffee was the best we have made. [[Operations/Facilities]] has a quote for a reinforced
enclosure. The quote is more than this line has ever earned.
