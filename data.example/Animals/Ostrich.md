# Ostrich

The only supplier that does part of our processing for us.

## The gastrolith advantage

An ostrich has no teeth. It swallows stones, which sit in the gizzard and grind its food
mechanically. Coffee cherry fed to an ostrich comes back **already ground**.

This is a real saving. We skip a grinding step entirely, and the grind happens before
roasting rather than after, which does something to the extraction that our cupping panel
has not finished arguing about.

## The grit problem

The stones do not stay in the ostrich.

Every batch has to be screened, twice, and even then our QC rejection rate on this line
runs at 11% against a roster average of 3%. One of those rejections was a customer
complaint, which is the worst possible place to find a small rock. The screening protocol
is in [[Operations/Quality-Control]].

Particle size is also inconsistent in a way no burr grinder would tolerate. We sell this
line as a cafetière grind and decline all requests for espresso.

## Note

An ostrich can kick forward with enough force to break a fence rail. Retrieval is a
two-person job with a barrier, same policy as [[Animals/Elephant]].
