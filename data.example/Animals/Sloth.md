# Sloth

The slowest product in the portfolio, in every sense.

## The fermentation

A sloth takes up to two weeks to digest a single meal. This is not a marketing
exaggeration, it is the actual transit time, and it does what a very long ferment always
does: deep, winey, almost fruit-leather notes, very low acidity, and a finish that goes on
much longer than it should.

Cuppers either love it or describe it as "compost". There is no middle group. See
[[Brand/Tasting-Notes]].

## The operational problem

Our lead time from feeding to retrieval is 9 to 14 days and we cannot narrow it. The sloth
also descends to ground level only about once a week, which is when retrieval is possible
at all.

This means:

- Demand forecasting has to run two weeks ahead of a product nobody can predict demand for.
- A single missed window pushes an order out by another week.
- Expedited shipping on a product called **Slow Press** has drawn comment.

## Status

Small-batch only, sold by allocation. [[Operations/Supply-Chain]] treats it as a
seasonal line rather than stock.
