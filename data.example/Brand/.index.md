## 📂 Files in this folder

- **Naming.md** — Product naming: the approved line names, the long list of rejected ones, and the three rules the rejections produced about never naming the mechanism, never being cute about the animal, and never using a word that makes the customer picture the process.
- **Tasting-Notes.md** — The tasting vocabulary problem: an approved descriptor list, a banned list of words that are accurate but unsellable, and the standing rule that a note must describe the cup rather than the journey the bean took to reach it.
