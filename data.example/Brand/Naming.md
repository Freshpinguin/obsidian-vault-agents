# Naming

The company name was the easy part. **Second Pass Coffee** was approved in twenty minutes
and has never been questioned.

## Approved line names

| Line | Name | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Capybara house blend | **Still Waters** | Calm, accurate, sells |
| Bat single origin | **Night Harvest** | Legally reviewed, see [[Animals/Bat]] |
| Sloth allocation | **Slow Press** | Honest to the point of a complaint |
| Elephant tins | **Grey Reserve** | Dignified, expensive-sounding |
| Goat heritage | **Kaldi's Own** | Carries the legend from [[Animals/Goat]] |

## Rejected, with reasons

- **Gut Instinct** — rejected. Everybody loved it. Legal pointed out it names the
  mechanism in two words.
- **Bean There** — rejected on grounds of taste, and not the coffee kind.
- **The Second Breakfast** — implies the customer is the one doing the digesting.
- **Civet Minded** — we do not sell civet coffee. See [[Animals/Civet]].
- **Pre-Owned Beans** — technically perfect, commercially fatal.
- **Doubly Processed** — approved for three days, then withdrawn when someone asked what
  the first processing was.
- **Badger's Choice** — withdrawn with the line. See [[Animals/Honey-Badger]].

## The three rules

1. Never name the mechanism.
2. Never be cute about the animal. The animal is a supplier, not a mascot.
3. If the name makes a reader picture the process, the name is dead.

Rule 3 has killed more good names than the other two combined.
