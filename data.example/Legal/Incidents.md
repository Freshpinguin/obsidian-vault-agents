# Incidents

Every entry here changed a policy. That is the only reason this log is worth keeping.

## INC-1 — First batch lost entirely

**Q1.** Our first finished batch was left in an open sack on the ground during a feeding
window. A goat ate it. The whole batch, plus the retrieval log, plus one clipboard.

**Policy change.** Nothing stays on the ground near a goat. Sealed containers only. See
[[Animals/Goat]].

## INC-2 — Handler injury, elephant line

**Q2.** A single handler attempted a retrieval alone during a herd movement. Bruised ribs,
no lasting injury, entirely avoidable.

**Policy change.** Two-person minimum for [[Animals/Elephant]], now in
[[Operations/Retrieval]].

## INC-3 — Neighbour complaint, upheld in part

**Q2.** Complaint concerning drying bed odour and prevailing wind. Upheld on the odour
point, dismissed on the animals.

**Policy change.** Site layout revised, hedge planted. See [[Operations/Facilities]] and
[[Legal/Regulatory]].

## INC-4 — Feed store breach

**Q3.** The honey badger opened the feed store. Then, on a second occasion, did not use
the door.

**Policy change.** Feed store relocated and reinforced.

## INC-5 — Class D quality failure

**Q3.** A batch reached the sorting table containing matter that was unambiguously class D
under [[Operations/Quality-Control]]. Root cause was a wash delayed past six hours during
a staffing gap.

**Policy change.** The four-hour wash limit became a hard stop. A batch past the limit is
destroyed, not downgraded. This is the most expensive rule we have and nobody argues with
it.

## INC-6 — Approach refused, with emphasis

**Q3.** A handler attempted retrieval near [[Animals/Honey-Badger]]. The animal made its
position clear. No injury, considerable noise.

**Policy change.** Line suspended pending the enclosure quoted in
[[Operations/Facilities]]. Third incident of the quarter, all the same supplier.

## INC-7 — Caffeinated penguins, three birds

**Q3.** During the cold-brew feeding trial, three of fourteen Humboldt penguins ate coffee
cherry. A penguin has no evolutionary exposure to caffeine and weighs about four kilograms.

Reported effects, from the park's own log rather than ours:

| Bird | Effect | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| P4 | Continuous swimming, described as "not typical" | 90 minutes |
| P7 | Sustained vocalisation audible from a neighbouring enclosure | 40 minutes |
| P11 | Left the enclosure by means never established | — |

No bird was harmed; the park vet confirmed this in writing, twice, at our request. The
keeper bitten on day ten of the trial declined to file anything and asked only that the
trial end.

**Policy change.** No animal is offered coffee cherry unless coffee cherry is a plausible
part of what it already eats. This sounds obvious written down. It was not obvious in the
meeting where the line was approved.

Line abandoned. See [[Animals/Penguin]] for the full post-mortem and
[[Meetings/Decisions]], ADR-5.
