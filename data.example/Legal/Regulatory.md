# Regulatory

This product sits inside three frameworks simultaneously, and none of them was written
with it in mind.

## 1. Food hygiene

The governing question is not where the bean has been. It is what happens after. Our
process is wash, triple rinse, dry, then roast at 210°C for eleven minutes, which is well
past the point where the hygiene question is settled.

The inspector's exact words were "the roast handles it", followed by a long pause.

Documented limits are in [[Operations/Retrieval]] and they are not negotiable.

## 2. Animal welfare

The binding constraint on the whole business.

- Every animal is fed on its own schedule. No force-feeding, ever. This is why we do not
  keep [[Animals/Civet]].
- No animal may be confined for production purposes.
- Retrieval may not involve handling the animal. The two-person rule exists for the
  handlers, not the animals.

This is audited annually. We have passed three times.

## 3. Novel food labelling

Unresolved, and we are being careful rather than clever. We do not claim a health
benefit, we do not claim digestion where there is none (see [[Animals/Bat]]), and we keep
the approved descriptor list in [[Brand/Tasting-Notes]] as the labelling boundary.
