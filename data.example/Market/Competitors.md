# Competitors

Two groups. Only one of them worries us.

## The established producers

Kopi Luwak at scale, and the Black Ivory elephant operation. Both are older, better
known and better capitalised than we are.

They do not worry us, because they have one animal each. Our roster is the product. A
customer who has had civet coffee has had civet coffee; a customer who has had our house
blend still has eight tins they have not tried. See [[Market/Customer-Research]], the
collector segment.

## The lab ferment startups

These worry us.

Several groups now reproduce the enzymatic ferment of digestion in a tank: the same enzyme
families, the same pH curve, controlled temperature, no animal. Early results are good.
One of them is already selling.

| Factor | Them | Us |
|---|---|---|
| Yield | near 100% | 4:1 to 33:1 |
| Consistency | batch-controlled | animal-dependent |
| Retrieval hours per kg | 0 | 3 to 22 |
| Ethics questions | none | an ongoing conversation |
| Story | none | the entire business |

They win every column but the last one, and the last one is the one customers pay for.
Our own blind tasting in [[Market/Customer-Research]] suggests the flavour gap is already
gone, which means we are not a coffee company competing on coffee. We are a story company
with a very demanding supply chain.

This is logged as the company's main strategic risk and discussed in
[[Meetings/Decisions]], ADR-7.
