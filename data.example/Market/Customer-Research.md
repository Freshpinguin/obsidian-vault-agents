# Customer Research

Three segments, and only one of them drinks the coffee.

## Segments

| Segment | Share of orders | Repeat rate | What they want |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gift buyers | 48% | 4% | A story and a tin |
| Collectors | 19% | 22% | The one they have not tried |
| Coffee drinkers | 33% | 40% | A good cup, consistently |

## The novelty trap

Half our revenue comes from people who will never buy again, and they are the cheapest to
acquire and the most enthusiastic to talk about us. This is a very pleasant way to not
build a business.

Everything strategic we do is aimed at the third segment, who do not care about the animal
at all, and who are the only reason [[Animals/Capybara]] is 70% of revenue.

## The blind tasting

We ran it, against our own advice.

Fifty people, six cups, no animals named, one of them a good washed Ethiopian with no
animal involved whatsoever. The Ethiopian placed second. [[Animals/Bat]] placed first.
[[Animals/Civet]] placed fifth.

The finding is that our customers can taste quality and cannot taste digestion. Nobody has
decided what to do with this, and ADR-7 in [[Meetings/Decisions]] formally defers it.
