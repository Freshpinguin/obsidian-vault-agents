# Pricing

We price off labour, not ingredients. The cherry is nearly free. The retrieval is not.

## The floor

Floor price per kilogram is retrieval hours times loaded labour cost, plus processing,
plus a rejection allowance from [[Operations/Quality-Control]]. For
[[Animals/Elephant]], the floor alone lands above 1,000 EUR.

## The ceiling

Set entirely by [[Animals/Civet]]. Kopi Luwak at 600 EUR per kilogram taught the market
what this category costs, and no amount of superior flavour lets us go far past it without
the customer deciding we are taking liberties.

This is awkward, because our best products have the worst yields.

## The decoy finding

The elephant line does not make money and is not supposed to. With it on the menu, house
blend sales rise by about a third: next to 1,400 EUR, a 180 EUR bag reads as the sensible
choice. This is the single most reliable effect in our sales data and it is documented in
[[Market/Customer-Research]].
