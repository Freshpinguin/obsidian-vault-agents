# Facilities

One site, four buildings, and a planning application we are glad we filed early.

## Buildings

| Building | Purpose | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Wash house | Stages 1 to 3 of processing | Downwind, by design |
| Drying shed | Raised beds, covered | Shares a wall with nothing |
| Roastery | Roast and pack | Deliberately separate |
| Office | Two desks and a kettle | Not connected to the kettle in the roastery |

## Why the roastery is a separate building

Roasting smells excellent. Everything before roasting does not. When both happened in one
building, the excellent smell arrived carrying the other one, and our first tasting event
was held in a room where nobody finished a cup.

The separation cost more than the roaster. It was the best money this company has spent.

## The neighbour complaint

One complaint, Q2, upheld in part. It concerned the drying beds and the wind direction,
not the animals. The remedy was a hedge and a revised layout, both done.

The complaint is logged in [[Legal/Incidents]] and referenced in [[Legal/Regulatory]] as
our only open compliance item.
