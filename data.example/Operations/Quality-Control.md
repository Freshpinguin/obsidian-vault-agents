# Quality Control

Cupping protocol is standard SCA. Everything before the cupping is not.

## Defect classes

| Class | Definition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| A | Foreign matter, organic | Reject batch |
| B | Foreign matter, mineral (grit, stone) | Re-screen, re-grade |
| C | Off-flavour from slow wash | Downgrade to blend |
| D | Anything identifiable as having been part of the animal | **Stop line, log incident** |

Class D has occurred once. It is in [[Legal/Incidents]] and it is the reason the four-hour
wash limit in [[Operations/Retrieval]] is a hard limit and not a target.

## Screening

Every line is screened once. [[Animals/Ostrich]] is screened twice, because of the
gastroliths, and still runs an 11% rejection rate against a 3% roster average.

## Rejection rates

| Animal | Rejection rate |
|---|---|
| Capybara | 2% |
| Elephant | 4% |
| Sloth | 9% |
| Ostrich | 11% |

## Cupping panel

Three cuppers, blind, no animal named. This matters: when the animal is named the scores
move by up to 6 points in either direction depending on the cupper's feelings about that
animal. The [[Animals/Bat]] line scores highest blind and mid-table named, which tells us
something uncomfortable about the whole premise.
