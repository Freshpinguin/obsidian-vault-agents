# Retrieval

The step everyone asks about and nobody wants to write down. This is the written-down
version.

## Vocabulary policy

Internally the word is **retrieval**. In customer-facing material it is **collection**.
Nobody says the other words, not because they are inaccurate, but because the sentence
stops being about coffee the moment they appear. See [[Brand/Tasting-Notes]].

## The procedure

1. **Feeding window.** Ripe cherry is offered on the animal's own schedule. No animal in
   the roster is fed on ours. This is both an ethics requirement from
   [[Legal/Regulatory]] and the only thing that works.
2. **Transit wait.** Species-dependent. Capybara 1 to 2 days, ostrich under a day, sloth
   up to 14. The table lives in [[Operations/Supply-Chain]].
3. **Retrieval.** Two people minimum for [[Animals/Elephant]], [[Animals/Ostrich]] and
   anything on the suspended list. One person is permitted for
   [[Animals/Capybara]] only.
4. **Wash and sort.** Within four hours. This is a flavour limit, not a hygiene one;
   hygiene has its own limit, which is much shorter.

## Time limits

| Step | Limit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Retrieval to first wash | 4 h | Off-flavours develop fast |
| First wash to final rinse | 30 min | Three rinses, no shortcuts |
| Final rinse to drying bed | 1 h | Mould risk |

## Hours per kilogram

This is the number the business runs on and the number nobody believes.

| Animal | Retrieval hours per kg |
|---|---|
| Capybara | 3 |
| Ostrich | 7 |
| Sloth | 16 |
| Elephant | 22 |

An espresso machine costs less than a week of elephant retrieval. See [[Market/Pricing]].
